US data from QFinVerse

On assay.qfinverse.com, US universes read the daily panel store that QFinVerse builds from MASSIVE: end-of-day bars from September 2003, splits and dividends, and the MASSIVE ticker reference. Assay does not ingest US data itself in this setup.

Setup

export ASSAY_QFV_PANEL_DIR=/srv/qfinverse/data/daily_panel   # the store's root (holds CURRENT)
assay serve-api

The reader is vendored in src/assay/data/qfv_panel (format qfv-daily-panel/1); scripts/sync_qfv_panel.py copies it again from a QFinVerse checkout. QFinVerse updates the store every trading day at 21:45 New York time; Assay switches to the new version on its next request, and results cached for the old version are not reused.

Universes

Universe Members
TOP500, TOP1000, TOP3000 most liquid common stocks on the first trading day of each month
SP500, NASDAQ100 dated index histories, matched to QFinVerse securities

The TOP universes rank by median dollar volume over the previous 63 trading days (at least 50 days traded, last close at least $5). ETFs, ADRs, preferreds, warrants, units and rights are left out. Membership uses only earlier data and keeps securities that later delisted.

An evaluation covers every security that was a member at any time in the period and scores it only on the days it was a member. A universe read from the members on the last day would leave out every stock that dropped out; on NASDAQ100 over 2016–2026 that flips the sign of low-volatility and range factors.

Securities

Bars carry tickers only, so each security has a permanent id:

  • a ticker that comes back after more than 63 trading days without a bar is a new security; the older one is labelled TICKER~YYYY-MM (for example ABC~2004-03);
  • renames are one security when the MASSIVE ticker history or a shared composite FIGI says so (FB → META), or when a delisted and a new common stock share a CIK and the price carries on within 25% (COH → TPR).

A ticker in an expression or a chart means the security that held it on the last day of the window.

Corporate actions

Prices are stored as traded and adjusted when read, onto the share basis of the last day of the window. Only events with an ex-date inside the window are applied, so a backtest that ends in the past never sees a later split.

Field Meaning
open high low close split- and dividend-adjusted (total return basis)
volume split-adjusted
transactions as reported
dvol close × volume, as traded
ret daily total return, the same in every window

Every split is checked against the prices before it is used:

Status Meaning Applied
ok prices jump by the split ratio on the ex-date yes
moved the jump is a few days off the vendor's date; the split moves there yes
no_jump no such jump within 10 trading days: already adjusted, or never happened no
small ratio within 10% of 1:1 (stock dividends) yes

Dividends are applied as 1 − cash / previous close. A dividend is skipped when it is not paid in USD, when it is more than half the price (nearly always a unit error), or when there is no close in the 15 days before the ex-date.

Speed

Panels are dense float32 arrays by year, memory-mapped. TOP500 over 2016–2026 loads in about 0.3 s and all of TOP3000 over 2010–2026 in about 3 s. Six factors on SP500 over ten years evaluate in about 11 s, against 54 s on the per-ticker parquet store.
